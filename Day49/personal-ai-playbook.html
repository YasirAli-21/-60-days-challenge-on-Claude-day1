What is this tool?

The AI Playbook turns your recurring AI tasks into reusable, editable workflow templates — instead of writing a prompt from scratch every time, you fill in a few variables and get a consistently well-structured prompt. It also includes two builders for making your own prompts from scratch.

Dashboard — browse ready-made workflows for learning, building, and content creation. Prompt Builder — assemble a custom prompt from labeled building blocks (role, objective, constraints…). Loop Builder — turn any prompt into a self-checking, iterating "run until done" prompt. My Workflows — everything you've saved, favorited, or built yourself, stored on this device.

Loop Builder

A "loop prompt" instructs the AI to check its own output against a goal, improve it, and repeat — instead of stopping after one draft. Useful for code that must pass tests, or writing that must hit a bar. Define the five pieces below and it assembles into one prompt you paste in.

Loop definition

Base task: the normal, single-shot prompt you want turned into a loop.

🎯 Goal — what "done" looks like

The concrete finish line. Without this, the AI won't know when to stop looping.

📏 Evaluation criteria — how each attempt is judged

The checklist the AI scores its own draft against on every pass.

🔁 Improvement strategy — how it should revise

Tells the AI *how* to iterate — e.g. fix the single biggest issue first, or rewrite fully each time.

🛑 Stop conditions — when to stop looping

Prevents infinite looping. Always include a max-attempts cap as a safety net.

🛡 Safety rules — guardrails during looping

Keeps unattended iteration from going off the rails — no scope creep, no fabricated results.

What is this?

Quick orientation

The AI Playbook is a personal system for reusing your best prompts instead of rewriting context every time you open a chat. It has four parts:

▤ Dashboard

Ready-made workflow templates grouped by what you actually use AI for: learning ML concepts, building/debugging projects, and creating content. Each has a fill-in-the-blanks template, an example, and best practices.

⚙ Prompt Builder

Build a prompt from labeled pieces — Role, Objective, Context, Constraints, Reasoning Strategy, Output Format, Tone, Examples, Quality Checks — instead of one wall of text. Good for one-off but important prompts.

↻ Loop Builder

Turns a normal prompt into one that makes the AI check its own work against a goal and keep improving until it's done (or hits a safety limit). Good for coding and iterative writing tasks.

★ My Workflows

Anything you favorite, duplicate, or build is saved here, in your browser's local storage only.
